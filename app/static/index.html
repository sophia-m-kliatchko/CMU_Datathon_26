Each facility comes with its evidence level, where that evidence comes from, and what we couldn't confirm.

T1
A current certification for this service (LaQshya, or a service-specific NABH accreditation).
T2
The same kind of certification, but expired or no longer current.
T3
Indirect evidence: specialist training approval, network membership, the trauma-centre list, or the facility's own listing.
R
Service evidence was found, but it isn't yet confirmed to belong to this facility (match under review).
U
No evidence found. That doesn't mean the service isn't offered.
X
A credible, dated, independent record says the service isn't available.
